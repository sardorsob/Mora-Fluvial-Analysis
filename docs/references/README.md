# References

- R. D. (Dan) Moore (2004), *Introduction to Salt Dilution Gauging for Streamflow Measurement, Part 2: Constant-rate Injection*. Streamline Watershed Management Bulletin, 8(1), pp. 11–15.
- R. D. (Dan) Moore (2005), *Introduction to Salt Dilution Gauging for Streamflow Measurement, Part III: Slug Injection Using Salt in Solution*. Streamline Watershed Management Bulletin, 8(2), pp. 1–6.
- Mark E. Richardson (2015), *Refinement of tracer dilution methods for discharge measurements in steep mountain streams*. University of British Columbia.
- Mark Richardson, Gabe Sentlinger, Dan Moore and André Zimmermann (2017), *Quantifying the Relation between Electrical Conductivity and Salt Concentration for Dilution Gauging Via Dry Salt Injection*. [Article and DOI: 10.22230/jwsm.2017v1n1a1](https://doi.org/10.22230/jwsm.2017v1n1a1).

Moore (2004) describes constant-rate injection; Moore (2005) describes solution-slug injection. Richardson's thesis and the 2017 paper inform mass-stock calibration, temperature/probe comparability and uncertainty. The repository's implemented calculation is specified in [calibration](../calibration.md); a cited method is not necessarily implemented or validated here.
